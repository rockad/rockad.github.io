Personal Workspace App

A personal local command-line and MCP integration for Aleksandr Korolev's personal Gmail and Google Calendar workflows.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Email

About

This tool runs entirely on its owner's own workstation. It is a single-user utility - not a public service, and not a hosted product. It exists so that its owner can read mail, manage drafts, and review calendar events from the terminal.

Privacy

Details about which Google API scopes are used, where data is stored, and how access can be revoked are documented in the Privacy Policy.
